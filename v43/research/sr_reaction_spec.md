# Support/resistance reaction v1 — exploratory
Separate Upbit and Bitstamp daily OHLC, through 2026-11-26 UTC. Positive consistent OHLC, reset on missing dates. Confirm 10% close reversal pivots; support from confirmed low close, resistance from confirmed high close, only available next bar. Each prediction time is every 10th closed daily bar within continuous segment after 180 contiguous days; select nearest prior confirmed support below close or resistance above close (within 2% to 20% distance, pivot age <=365 days). Compare equally defined placebo levels reflected in log-distance of opposite-side nearest pivot: for support use close^2 / resistance; for resistance use close^2 / support. Require both sides eligible at the same origin; placebo and real distance may differ, report distance and stratify (2–5,5–10,10–20% absolute fractional distance); additionally compare paired origins with real/placebo distance ratio within 0.8–1.25. Placebo may coincide with true pivot, report. Each zone +/- 0.5% of level. First touch within next 10 daily bars, if gap/missing before touch censor, else no touch. Upon touch, evaluate next 5 full daily bars only: reaction 3% favorable from level vs break 3% adverse from level, whichever first; if both in one bar order unknown; if touch bar already reaches either barrier classify touch-bar ambiguous (no intrabar ordering). Missing/future bars censor. Origins every 10 bars may overlap during response phase, report raw and independent sample using every 20th origin (even origin index); do not pool markets. Assess visit rates, reaction/break/no outcome/ambiguous/censor, resolved reaction fraction, matched distance cohort, 2023+ reused evaluation vs earlier descriptive. No claim of actual volume profile, no model promotion; screening criterion >=5 percentage point improvement in resolved reaction fraction over placebo in both markets, each side, >=30 resolved in each arm of matched independent evaluation. No tuning; no calibrated probability. This tests support/resistance conditional reaction, not forecast of next extreme.
